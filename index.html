Why this shape

Design for the failure mode, not the demo.

01 / Source-aware

Every source gets a behavior profile.

Rate limits, schema drift, latency, and block signals are treated as routing inputs — not noise in a generic retry loop.

02 / Checkpointed

Make “resume” a first-class state.

Persist progress at meaningful boundaries so a blocked source does not force a full replay downstream.

03 / Honest

Show operators what actually happened.

No magic status badge. The control plane explains the route decision and leaves a trace that can be audited.

A product, not a promise
